# Yahoo Finance and Twelve Data provider assessment

Reviewed 27 November 2026 for [plugins #8](https://github.com/tailoralm/personal-ledger-plugins/issues/8) against [Personal Ledger provider SDK v1 at f145429](https://github.com/tailoralm/Personal-Ledger/blob/f145429702c100bdeeb5b4311b92f53db6bb0f13/docs/market-data-provider-v1.md). Both providers are blocked. This assessment adds no live provider packages or catalog entries.

## Yahoo Finance

Yahoo's US Terms of Service require express prior permission for automated access or collection. The Yahoo developer terms cover documented Yahoo APIs and their applicable product terms. This review established neither express permission nor an authorized, supported Finance REST route with a credential compatible with the broker. The Yahoo Finance integration therefore has no verified quota, SLA, market or history entitlement, or license to retain price observations in Ledger and its backups. Reopen only with express permission covering automation, the exact supported routes and authentication, US and European listing coverage, and the rights to store, display and retain observations. [Yahoo Terms of Service](https://legal.yahoo.com/us/en/yahoo/terms/otos/index.html?.intl=us&.lang=en-US), [Yahoo Developer API Terms](https://legal.yahoo.com/us/en/yahoo/terms/product-atos/apiforydn/index.html)

## Twelve Data

**Authentication blocks installation.** Twelve Data documents two REST key transports on `https://api.twelvedata.com`, the `apikey` query parameter and `Authorization: apikey KEY`. The pinned broker injects only `Authorization: Bearer KEY` or one declared lower-case `x-*` key header on an approved literal HTTPS GET route. The official REST docs list no interoperable header option. Twelve Data's hosted MCP uses a separate OAuth flow outside the plugin protocol. The broker would need an explicit, host-owned `Authorization: apikey` credential type, unless Twelve Data documents compatible authentication for the same first-party REST routes. [Twelve Data REST authentication](https://twelvedata.com/auth.md), [API introduction](https://twelvedata.com/docs/introduction/libraries), [Pinned network contract](https://github.com/tailoralm/Personal-Ledger/blob/f145429702c100bdeeb5b4311b92f53db6bb0f13/docs/market-data-provider-v1.md#network-and-credentials)

**Plan and feed entitlement.** Basic lists US equities and ETFs, forex and crypto with internal non-display use. The default US real-time equity feed covers venues representing about 5% of US trading volume. The US guide lists consolidated historical and EOD data from Basic, available after midnight Eastern Time on the next trading day. Consolidated real-time and OTC data require separate licensing. Grow lists internal display and global EOD equities and ETFs. The exchange matrix lists Lisbon, Paris, Xetra and London EOD on individual Grow, with other exchanges at higher tiers. Pro advertises real-time EU coverage, and the live exchange matrix lists Cboe Europe real-time as an add-on. Confirm the actual MIC, security, feed, delay, endpoint and display entitlement for the account. A US-listed ETF holding European assets is still a US listing. [Individual plan comparison](https://twelvedata.com/pricing), [US equities guide](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/9935903-us-equities-market-data), [Exchange matrix](https://twelvedata.com/exchanges), [EOD coverage](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/12682324-end-of-day-eod-pricing-market-data)

**Storage and display.** Terms §§2, 3 and 16 limit internal storage and cache duration to the subscription, documentation and third-party rights. External display and redistribution require the applicable authorization and may carry attribution and exchange obligations. The terms require deletion within 30 days after expiration or termination, with a limited compliance audit exception. Confirm display rights, retention duration, backup/export rules and a workable deletion procedure before enabling durable ingestion. Core persists normalized observations and cache state. Its job cache TTL controls reuse, and its backup and restore flow can retain observations. Clearing credentials or turning off scheduling does not purge historical data. Canonical decimal conversion preserves reconstructible prices, so it cannot confer derived-data rights. [Twelve Data Terms of Use](https://twelvedata.com/terms), [Commercial and personal usage](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5332349-commercial-and-personal-usage), [Pinned persistence and broker guide](https://github.com/tailoralm/Personal-Ledger/blob/f145429702c100bdeeb5b4311b92f53db6bb0f13/docs/market-data-provider-v1.md)

### Implementation criteria after the blockers clear

- `GET /symbol_search` searches names and tickers at one credit per request. Use the upstream ticker with a distinct MIC or exchange selector where the endpoint accepts one. Resolve prefix/suffix aliases against upstream listing metadata before approving a mapping. The published `isin` input on endpoints such as `/quote` and `/time_series` is available on all plans for entitled listings. Returning ISIN fields requires the ISIN Data add-on. Preserve each cross-listing's identity, currency and timezone even when several listings share an ISIN. [Symbol search](https://twelvedata.com/docs/discovery/symbol-search), [Quote](https://twelvedata.com/docs/market-data/quote), [Time series](https://twelvedata.com/docs/market-data/time-series), [October 2026 ISIN clarification](https://twelvedata.com/news/oct-2026-update)
- `GET /quote` supplies quote metadata and a timestamp. `GET /time_series` supplies historical OHLCV, with daily bars in exchange-local time and up to 5,000 points upstream. Use `/earliest_timestamp` to verify depth for the instrument and interval. Submit bounded daily windows with `enqueueBatch` and `rangeDays` to fit core's 1,000-item and 1 MiB limits. Test inclusive boundaries because date-only end bounds can omit the end session. Accept only the requested interval and range, keep gaps empty, and let core own any bounded cursor chain. Where supported, set `adjust=none`, `splits` or `all` explicitly for raw, split-adjusted or split-and-distribution-adjusted history. Preserve exchange-local market dates and use a verified upstream timestamp or known session close with DST and early closures. For confirmed pence listings, convert GBp/GBX to GBP by an exact decimal shift of two places before comparing against approved listing currency. Verify this using a London listing and a separate GBP-denominated listing. [Time series](https://twelvedata.com/docs/market-data/time-series), [Historical ranges](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5214728-getting-historical-data), [Historical depth](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5656039-how-to-get-historical-prices), [Minor currency units](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/12383853-why-are-some-securities-quoted-in-smaller-currency-units)
- Basic has 8 API credits per minute and 800 per UTC day, resetting at the start of each minute and at 00:00 UTC. Paid quotas use the subscribed credits per minute with no daily cap. `/quote` and `/time_series` cost one credit per symbol, so a partially filled batch costs less upstream than a full batch. Core v1 charges a static per-operation credit cost on every dispatched attempt, including retries and pages. Start with batch size one for exact local costs on these endpoints. Core can enforce conservative rolling minute limits and the UTC day boundary using editable `configureQueue` credit rules, with concurrency one as the initial setting. Core cannot reconcile `api-credits-used`, `api-credits-left` or usage from other clients sharing the key. The guest sees HTTP status, body and validated `Retry-After`, and core handles HTTP 429 before normalization. A rate-limit error in a successful HTTP envelope needs explicit normalization. Test entitlement errors, malformed payloads and the distinction between empty success and incomplete or invalid market data. [Credits and reset rules](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5615854-credits), [Usage headers](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5713553-control-over-api-usage), [API errors](https://twelvedata.com/docs/introduction/errors), [Pinned broker guide](https://github.com/tailoralm/Personal-Ledger/blob/f145429702c100bdeeb5b4311b92f53db6bb0f13/docs/market-data-provider-v1.md#durable-broker-and-schedules-64)
- Keep schedules manual by default. A user can configure a local calendar recurrence after checking when the entitled feed publishes EOD bars. A daily reference time of 00:00 in the market timezone can be moved later to allow publication and resolve overlapping workloads. Upstream quota resets and publication schedules are independent. Core's dated schedule templates carry literal dates. Rolling history refreshes require a host caller that computes dates and submits the requests. [Pinned scheduling guide](https://github.com/tailoralm/Personal-Ledger/blob/f145429702c100bdeeb5b4311b92f53db6bb0f13/docs/market-data-provider-v1.md#durable-broker-and-schedules-64)

## Alternative for US daily history

FinzData documents `GET https://api.finzdata.com/v1/prices/daily` with `symbol`, `start` and `end` query parameters, JSON by default, and `X-API-Key`, which the broker can declare as `x-api-key`. The Free plan lists 12 months of daily US stock history and 1,000 calls per day. The paid Look-back plan lists daily bars for NYSE and Nasdaq stocks, including delisted names, plus 231 US-listed ETFs and a purchased history window starting at five years. It includes unlimited API calls and downloadable history that remains available after stopping paid updates. Use that narrower plan-specific ETF list when assessing entitlement because FinzData also advertises a wider ETF catalog. Check the exact tickers, adjustment mode and session timestamp before treating an ETF response as canonical history. This endpoint covers US daily history. The reviewed sources establish no equivalent entitlement for European listings, ISIN lookup or real-time quotes. [FinzData REST docs](https://finzdata.com/docs), [Plan comparison](https://finzdata.com/pricing), [Daily dataset](https://finzdata.com/datasets)

FinzData's standard Look-back license covers one individual's own research and trading and allows creating personal analysis and charts. Paid history may be kept after cancellation of updates. The terms prohibit sharing raw or reconstructible data and require deletion when a license ends. Institutional covers internal business use within its order or contract. A separately scoped US-history provider is technically viable for evaluation. Before distributing one, confirm that the order covers personal portfolio valuation, private backups, retained update data, and any third-party source restrictions, or obtain written terms covering that use. This assessment verified the published routes and entitlements without a live account. [FinzData Terms §§3–6 and 12](https://finzdata.com/terms)
